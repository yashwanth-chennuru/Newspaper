Formula E secures Disney+ as a major global streaming home

The championship says every Gen4 session will stream live across 144 territories through Disney+ and ESPN channels.

Formula E says Disney+ will become a global streaming home for the championship from the start of the Gen4 era. The agreement covers 144 territories and includes the UK; viewers in the United States will also have ESPN+ as part of the offering.

The proposed coverage is comprehensive: live practice, qualifying and races rather than only race-day broadcasts. That is a meaningful distinction for a series whose technical and strategic narratives often begin well before the race.

Formula E says the Disney deal will operate alongside its existing free-to-air and linear-broadcast partners. Local rights and exact availability will therefore still need checking by market.

Chief executive Jeff Dodds described it as a major statement of intent as the all-electric series enters its fourth technical era. The Gen4 car is expected to be the championship’s fastest and most advanced to date.

For fans, the value is potentially simpler access to a full weekend in one familiar streaming environment. For Formula E, the deal gives the Gen4 launch a larger distribution platform—but its practical success will depend on discovery, local packaging and audience retention.
