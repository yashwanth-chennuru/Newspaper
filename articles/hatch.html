Meta reportedly prepares Hatch, a waitlisted consumer agent

Reported code signals point to research, creation and shopping workflows with a possible social-graph advantage.

TestingCatalog reports that Meta is preparing a consumer-facing autonomous agent called Hatch. The evidence cited is code and product-surface signals rather than an official launch, so the project’s exact form and timeline remain unconfirmed.

The reported task list is wide: image and video generation, shopping flows, learning and research, scheduled work, and file creation. That would position Hatch alongside the growing crop of agents designed to carry out multi-step tasks instead of merely answering prompts.

The distinctive possibility is social grounding. The report suggests Hatch could draw more deeply on Facebook and Instagram surfaces for feed exploration, creator discovery and shopping research than current Meta AI experiences do.

A waitlist appears to be part of the preparation, which could mean an initially constrained release. Reports cited by TestingCatalog also point to internal testing environments patterned after services such as Reddit, Etsy and DoorDash.

If it arrives as described, Hatch’s differentiator will not only be task execution but access to Meta’s consumer ecosystem. That remains a hypothesis, not a confirmed product promise—and social context will make privacy and user control especially important.
